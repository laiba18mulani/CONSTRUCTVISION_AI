📖 OVERVIEW

Bricks are one of the oldest and most trusted construction
materials used in civil engineering. They are manufactured
from clay, fly ash, concrete, or similar materials and are
widely used for walls, partitions, foundations, pavements,
and landscaping.

Bricks provide excellent compressive strength, thermal
insulation, fire resistance, and durability, making them an
essential material in residential and commercial buildings.

💡 Fun Fact:
The world's oldest known bricks were made over 9,000 years
ago and are still found in ancient civilizations!
