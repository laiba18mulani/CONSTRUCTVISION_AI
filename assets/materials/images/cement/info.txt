📖 OVERVIEW

Cement is the primary binding material used in construction.
When mixed with sand, aggregates, and water, it forms concrete
that provides strength and durability to buildings.

From small houses 🏡 to skyscrapers 🏢 and bridges 🌉,
cement plays a vital role in every construction project.

💡 Fun Fact:
More than 4 billion tonnes of cement are produced worldwide every year!
