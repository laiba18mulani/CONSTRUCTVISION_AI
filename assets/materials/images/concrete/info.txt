📖 OVERVIEW

Concrete is the world's most widely used construction material.
It is made by mixing cement, sand, aggregates, and water to
create a strong and durable structure. Concrete is used in
foundations, columns, beams, slabs, bridges, dams, roads,
and high-rise buildings because of its excellent strength
and long service life.

💡 Fun Fact:
Concrete is the second most consumed material on Earth,
after water!

🌱 ENVIRONMENT

Concrete production generates carbon emissions mainly due to
cement manufacturing. Using fly ash, GGBS, recycled aggregates,
and green concrete technologies helps reduce environmental
impact and supports sustainable construction.
