📖 OVERVIEW

Paint is a protective and decorative coating applied to walls,
ceilings, wood, metal, and concrete surfaces. It enhances the
appearance of buildings while protecting them from moisture,
dust, sunlight, corrosion, fungi, and weather damage.

Modern paints are designed to be durable, washable,
water-resistant, and eco-friendly, making them an essential
part of every construction project.

💡 Fun Fact:
A quality exterior paint can protect a building for over
10 years while keeping it attractive!
