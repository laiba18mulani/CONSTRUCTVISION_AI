📖 OVERVIEW

Sand is a naturally occurring fine aggregate used in almost
every construction project. It fills the spaces between
cement and coarse aggregates, improving the strength,
workability, and durability of concrete and mortar.

Good quality sand ensures stronger structures, smoother
plaster finishes, and better bonding in masonry work.

💡 Fun Fact:
A typical residential building may use more than
100 tonnes of sand during construction!

🌱 ENVIRONMENT

Excessive river sand mining can damage rivers and ecosystems.
Using Manufactured Sand (M-Sand) helps conserve natural
resources while providing excellent construction quality.
