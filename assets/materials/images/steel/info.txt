📖 OVERVIEW

Steel is one of the strongest and most reliable construction
materials used in modern civil engineering. It provides high
tensile strength and supports reinforced concrete structures,
making buildings safer, stronger, and more durable.

From houses 🏡 to skyscrapers 🏢, bridges 🌉, and flyovers,
steel is the backbone of modern infrastructure.

💡 Fun Fact:
The Eiffel Tower contains over **7,000 tonnes of steel** and
has stood strong for more than 130 years!

🌱 ENVIRONMENT

Steel is one of the world's most recycled construction
materials. Recycling steel saves natural resources, reduces
energy consumption, and lowers carbon emissions compared to
producing new steel.
