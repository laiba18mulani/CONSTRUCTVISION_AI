📖 OVERVIEW

Tiles are durable finishing materials used for flooring,
walls, bathrooms, kitchens, balconies, staircases, and
decorative surfaces. They not only enhance the appearance
of a building but also provide protection against water,
dust, stains, and daily wear.

Available in countless colours, textures, and patterns,
tiles combine beauty with long-lasting performance.

💡 Fun Fact:
Modern vitrified tiles absorb less than **0.5% water**,
making them ideal for moisture-prone areas.
